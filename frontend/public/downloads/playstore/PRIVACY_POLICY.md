# Privacy Policy for ChickenCrew

**Last updated:** April 2026

ChickenCrew ("we", "us", "our") operates the ChickenCrew mobile application
and the website https://karthikachickencentre.shop (the "Service"). This
page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and
disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in
accordance with this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide and improve the
Service:

### 1.1 Personal Information
- **Name** — to identify your order
- **Phone number** — to contact you about the order
- **Delivery address** (optional) — to fulfill home delivery

### 1.2 Location Data
- **Precise location** (optional) — captured via your device GPS when you
  tap "Share live location" during checkout. Used exclusively by the
  delivery rider to navigate to you.
- We do **not** collect location in the background.
- You can deny this permission and enter a manual address instead.

### 1.3 Order Data
- Items ordered, quantities, total amount, payment status
- Razorpay transaction reference (for paid orders)

### 1.4 Admin / Rider Data (for shop staff only)
- Email and password via Supabase Authentication
- Session tokens

### 1.5 Technical Data (non-personal)
- Device type, OS version, app version
- Crash reports (if any)

---

## 2. How We Use Information

We use your information to:

- Process and fulfill your orders
- Contact you about your order (call / SMS)
- Enable rider delivery navigation
- Process payments via Razorpay (see their privacy policy separately)
- Improve the app's functionality and fix bugs
- Comply with legal obligations

We do **not** sell or share your personal data with third parties for
marketing purposes.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

We use the following trusted third-party services:

- **Supabase** — database and authentication (https://supabase.com/privacy)
- **Razorpay** — payment processing (https://razorpay.com/privacy/)
- **Google Maps** — navigation links for delivery riders

These services only receive the minimum data needed to function (e.g.,
Razorpay receives order amount & transaction info; Google Maps receives
coordinates when rider taps "Navigate").

---

## 4. Data Retention

- Order records are retained for 2 years for accounting purposes.
- Location data is retained only as long as the order is active. After
  delivery, the coordinates are kept with the order record but not used
  further.
- You can request deletion of your data any time by emailing
  `knair9843@gmail.com`.

---

## 5. Security

We take reasonable security measures to protect your data:

- All data is transmitted over HTTPS (encrypted in transit)
- Admin accounts use strong password authentication
- Payment data is never stored on our servers (handled by Razorpay)
- Access to the admin panel is restricted to authorized shop staff

---

## 6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** the data we have about you
- **Correct** inaccurate data
- **Delete** your data (we'll process within 7 working days)
- **Opt-out** of location sharing (just deny the permission prompt)

To exercise any of these rights, email **knair9843@gmail.com**.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not
knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has
provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

---

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this
page with an updated "Last updated" date. Significant changes will also be
communicated in the app.
