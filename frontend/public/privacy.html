Privacy Policy for ChickenCrew

Last updated: April 2026

ChickenCrew ("we", "us", "our") operates the ChickenCrew mobile application and the website karthikachickencentre.shop (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

  • Name — to identify your order
  • Phone number — to contact you about the order
  • Delivery address (optional) — to fulfill home delivery

1.2 Location Data

  • Precise location (optional) — captured via device GPS when you tap "Share live location" at checkout. Used exclusively by the delivery rider to navigate to you.
  • We do not collect location in the background.
  • You can deny this permission and enter a manual address instead.

1.3 Order Data

  • Items ordered, quantities, total amount, payment status
  • Razorpay transaction reference (for paid orders)

1.4 Admin / Rider Data (for shop staff only)

  • Email and password via Supabase Authentication
  • Session tokens

2. How We Use Information

We use your information to:

  • Process and fulfill your orders
  • Contact you about your order (call / SMS)
  • Enable rider delivery navigation
  • Process payments via Razorpay
  • Improve the app's functionality and fix bugs
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — database and authentication
  • Razorpay — payment processing
  • Google Maps — navigation links for delivery riders

These services only receive the minimum data needed to function.

4. Data Retention

  • Order records retained for 2 years for accounting purposes.
  • Location data retained only while the order is active.
  • Data deletion requests via knair9843@gmail.com are processed within 7 working days.

5. Security

  • All data transmitted over HTTPS (encrypted in transit)
  • Strong password authentication for admin accounts
  • Payment data never stored on our servers (handled by Razorpay)
  • Admin panel access restricted to authorized shop staff

6. Your Rights

  • Access the data we have about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data
  • Opt-out of location sharing

To exercise any rights, email knair9843@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
